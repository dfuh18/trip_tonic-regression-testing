== description ==

Lisbon has a Mediterranean climate (Köppen: Csa), characterised by mild, rainy winters and warm to hot, dry summers. The average annual temperature is 17.6 °C (63.7 °F), with average daily highs of 21.7 °C (71.1 °F) and lows of 13.6 °C (56.5 °F).
In the coldest month, January, daytime highs typically range from 14 to 19 °C (57 to 66 °F), while nighttime lows range from 5 to 10 °C (41 to 50 °F), with an average sea temperature of 16 °C (61 °F). In the warmest month, August, daytime highs typically range from 25 to 32 °C (77 to 90 °F), nighttime lows range from 14 to 20 °C (57 to 68 °F), and the average sea temperature is about 20 °C (68 °F).
The climate of Lisbon is moderate, largely due to its location in southwestern Europe, its proximity to the Azores High and the Atlantic Ocean. The highest temperature ever recorded in Lisbon was 44.0 °C (111.2 °F) on 4 August 2018, while the lowest temperature ever recorded was −1.5 °C (29 °F) on February 15, 1860; although other locations in its  metropolitan area have recorded lower temperatures, not being as affected by the urban heat island effect of the city centre, with Sintra and Setúbal having reached −4 °C (25 °F) and −5.1 °C (22.8 °F), respectively, both experiencing about 13 days of frost per year on average.
Lisbon receives about 793 mm (31.2 in) of precipitation annually, with the majority falling during the autumn and winter months. November and December are the wettest months, contributing to a third of the total annual rainfall. The dry season typically lasts from early June to mid-September, with July and August being the driest months. During this period, there are typically about 8 days with measurable precipitation.
The city has about 2,875 hours of sunshine per year, one of the highest amounts in Europe. August is the sunniest month, with sunshine occurring for approximately 80% of the daytime, while December is the dullest, with sunshine occurring only 51% of the time.
