**Project:** Master's thesis at the University of St.Gallen

**Contact:** gian.hofmann@student.unisg.ch

We appreciate your decision to take part in this project. Please read the text below carefully. We are happy to answer questions or provide additional information if required.

## What is the purpose of the study?

Automated tests for conversational AI assistants are usually built from simulated conversations. It is not well understood how closely those simulations resemble the way people actually use an application. This study compares interactions produced by real users with interactions produced by simulation, in order to assess an automated testing system developed as part of a Master's thesis.

## What is being investigated and how?

You will use TripTonic, a travel planning application in which an AI assistant called Tonic helps you assemble a trip. Your requests to Tonic, and what you do with its suggestions, are recorded by the application and later compared with simulated interactions.

Neither you nor the assistant's performance is the object of this study. What is being evaluated is the automated testing system, and your interactions serve as material for that evaluation.

## Who can participate in the study?

Participants must be 18 years of age or older and able to use a laptop, desktop or tablet computer. No previous experience with AI assistants or travel planning tools is required.

## What will taking part involve?

Participation takes approximately 15 minutes:

1. You create an account or sign in.
2. You read this information and give or decline consent.
3. You answer four background questions.
4. You create a trip and ask Tonic for help at least three times. You may give a thumbs up or down on its replies where you wish to; this is optional.
5. After your third request, four short questions appear once. You may answer or dismiss them.

You are free to continue using the application afterwards, or to stop at any point.

**Please do not enter personal or sensitive information into the chat.** Write your requests as you would to any travel assistant, without personal details you would not wish to have recorded.

## Are there any benefits from participating?

No compensation is provided. Participation is voluntary and unpaid.

## Are there possible disadvantages and/or risks in participating?

There are no significant risks to your physical or mental wellbeing.

The study collects personal data, including a copy of what you write to the assistant. Your messages are processed by an external AI provider within the European Union. When the assistant searches for places, the search terms are sent to Google Maps Platform, which may process them outside Switzerland and the European Union, including in the United States. The measures taken to protect your data are set out below.

If you plan a trip together with other people in the application, the other members of that trip can see the trip's chat, including your requests to Tonic and its replies. This is how the application works for all users and is not specific to the study. A trip you create alone has no other members.

## What are my rights during participation?

Your participation in this study is voluntary. You can withdraw your consent to participate at any time and without providing a reason, without any negative consequences. If at any point during the study you are asked to do something you do not wish to do or are asked a question you prefer not to answer, no one can force you to comply.

If you decline consent, nothing beyond the fact that you declined is recorded, your account is removed from the study, and you are signed out.

## What information is being collected?

**For research purposes:**

- The date and time of your consent, and the version of this text you agreed to
- Your answers to the four background questions: age, gender, highest education, and how often you use AI chat assistants
- Your answers to the four questions shown after your third request, including any free text you choose to write
- A copy of each message you send to Tonic, with the time it was sent and how long the reply took
- How the assistant produced each reply: which model version, how many calls it made, which tools it used and the search terms it passed to them, and whether any errors occurred
- Whether you accepted, rejected or did not react to each of the assistant's suggestions, and any thumbs up or down you gave
- Changes made to your trip plan, recording which fields changed and whether the change came from you or from the assistant. The titles and notes you write are not recorded
- The type of device you used, derived from your browser. No device fingerprinting takes place

**For administrative purposes:** your account email address, which is required to create an account.

**Not collected:** location or GPS data, camera or microphone access, contacts, screen or keystroke recording. No third-party analytics services receive any data.

## How will my data be used?

**Administrative and research data are held separately.** Your email address belongs to your user account and is needed to sign in. The research data comprises your questionnaire answers, your requests to the assistant, and what you did with its suggestions. It is identified by an account identifier rather than by your name or email address. Results are published in anonymous form only.

**When is the administrative data deleted?** Your email address is part of your account rather than of the study, and remains for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes it, together with all study data.

**Where is the research data stored, and who has access to it?** All data is stored in a database operated by Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden. The application and its interface are hosted by Render in Frankfurt, Germany. Administrative access to the database is held by the researcher and by one other person who works on the application. The second person's access is required in order to run the application and is not used for the research. You can view and download your own data at any time through *Export my data* on your profile page. If you plan a trip together with other people, the other members of that trip can see the trip's chat, including your requests to the assistant and its replies; this is how the application works for all users. The application itself has no administrative screen through which this content can be viewed.

**How is the research data used?** It is used for the Master's thesis described above and for no other purpose. Results are reported in aggregate: counts, distributions, rates and averages. Individual interactions may be described in general terms where this helps to explain a finding, but no text you have written is reproduced.

**How long is it kept?** Your data is retained for eight months from the date you give consent. After that, the link to your account and the text of your messages are removed automatically, and only anonymous records of the kinds of requests made and their outcomes remain.

**Is data passed to third parties?** Your messages, together with your trip context and recent chat history, are sent to Google's Vertex AI service in order to generate the assistant's replies, and after the study to produce automated quality ratings. This processing takes place on Google's European endpoint and remains within the European Union. When the assistant looks up places, the search terms are sent to Google Maps Platform; Google does not guarantee that these are processed within Europe, so they may be processed outside Switzerland and the European Union, including in the United States. This is the only part of the study where data may leave Europe, and it concerns search terms rather than your messages. The database and hosting providers named above process data on behalf of the researcher. No other party receives it.

## What are my rights regarding my data?

You have the right to request information about the personal data collected from you at any time. Until the point of deletion, you have the option to withdraw your consent for the processing of your personal data without providing a reason. Additionally, until the point of complete anonymization, you can request that the information you provided during the study be corrected, handed over to you, blocked from processing, or deleted. Please contact the designated contact person provided above for such requests.

You can remove all of your study data yourself by deleting your account on your profile page, which deletes it immediately and permanently. If you wish to have your study data deleted without deleting your account, please contact the researcher, who will remove it on request. Please write from the email address associated with your account, or include it in your message, so that your account can be identified. Data that has already been used to produce reported results cannot be withdrawn from those results, although the underlying records are still deleted.
